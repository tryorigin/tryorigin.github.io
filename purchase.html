CHECKOUT QUESTIONS

Before you check out

How do I receive my license key?

Checkout runs through Whop. Once payment completes, your license key is issued instantly to your Whop account and the email you checked out with.

Can I upgrade tiers later?

Yes — contact support with your existing key and you'll only pay the price difference to move up a tier.

Does one license cover multiple PCs?

Each license key is tied to one machine at a time. You can deactivate and re-activate on a new machine from your license dashboard.

What's the refund policy?

If your benchmarked FPS doesn't improve after running Origin, you're covered by a 7-day refund window — just reach out through Whop with your before/after benchmark.
